# OpenBMB-SOAR-2026
An attempt at an inference acceleration competition.

This is my attempt at the SOAR 2026 Sparse Operator Acceleration Competition hosted by OpenBMB (https://soar.openbmb.cn/), which I joined during the final three weeks.

In the end, I implemented W4A16 GPTQModel + Marlin quantization. However, the speedup was minimal, and I didn't have enough time to fix the calibration set, leaving the final performance at around 96% of the original accuracy.

Although this attempt was somewhat of a failure, I am sharing it here to document and remember everything I learned throughout the competition.

Due to the large file size, the file itself is not uploaded here. Instead, I've shared the logs and notes documented during the process.
